🛟 CyberDad Shield Kit help

How can we help?

Resend your download, fix the email on your order, or ask for a refund. Email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com and you'll hear back within 1 business day.

Resend my download

After checkout you'll see a "Download your kit" button on the confirmation page. We also email your downloads from ruel@getcyberdadkit.com: the order confirmation, plus "Your CyberDad Shield Kit is ready (download inside)" with two links, "Download your CyberDad Shield Kit" and "Download your 3 Fast Wins (start here)".

Can't find the email?

  1. Check Spam/Junk and, in Gmail, the Promotions tab.
  2. Search your inbox for "Shield Kit" or ruel@getcyberdadkit.com.
  3. Still nothing? Email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com with the email address you used at checkout, and we'll send your downloads directly.

Used the wrong email at checkout?

  1. Email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com from the address you'd like to use.
  2. Include the address you typed at checkout, your name as it appears on the order and the approximate date of purchase.
  3. We'll match the order and send your downloads to the right address.

Can't open the PDF?

You get two PDFs: the CyberDad Shield Kit and the 1-page 3 Fast Wins sheet to start with.

  • Open it in a browser (Chrome, Safari or Edge), or save it first and open it from Files/Downloads.
  • On iPhone/iPad: tap the file, then the Share icon, then Save to Files. On Android: open it from the Files app.
  • Still stuck? Email us the device and the exact error message.

Refunds

If it isn't right for your family, email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com within 30 days of purchase for a full refund of what you paid.

  1. Email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com within 30 days of purchase.
  2. Include the email address you used at checkout and your name as it appears on the order. A reason is welcome but never required.
  3. You'll hear back within 1 business day, and your refund goes back to your original payment method.
