What's included in a refresh

Mobile-first design

Built for the phone your customers are holding, then opened up for desktop.

Your real content

We reuse and tidy your existing text, services, hours and photos you own. Nothing replaced with filler.

HTTPS and speed

A valid security certificate and a lightweight static site that loads quickly.

Working contact path

Click-to-call, email, map link and a contact form that opens a pre-filled email to you — all tested.

"Get a quote" on every page

A "Get a quote" button in the header of every page that opens your contact form.

Google Business Profile check

At launch we check that the name, phone, hours and website link on your Google Business Profile match your new site, and tell you about any mismatches. We don't log in to or edit your profile.

Review link

A "Leave us a review" button that opens your own Google review page: a review link you can share with all your customers.

Search basics

Page titles, descriptions, business info markup and a link to your Google Business Profile.

Accessibility basics

Readable contrast, keyboard navigation and image descriptions. (We don't promise legal "ADA compliance".)

Not included: online stores, booking systems or logo design. Rewritten wording comes with Refresh Plus. We can quote other work separately in writing.

How it works — all by email

  1. Tell us about your site. Use the form below or the request assistant. A few short questions.
  2. Get a free concept. If it's a fit, we email you a free concept image of your new homepage. No obligation.
  3. Get a written scope and price. Exactly what's included, what isn't, and the timeline.
  4. Approve and pay. Reply to approve the scope, then pay by secure card link (Stripe).
  5. Send your content. A questionnaire covers pages, photos, hours and where form messages should go.
  6. Review the preview. About 7–10 business days (a target, not a promise) after we have your content, you get a private preview link and the revision rounds in your scope.
  7. Launch. About 3 business days (a target, not a promise) after you approve, we connect your domain without touching your email settings and send you a handoff note.

We don't do phone or video calls. Every question gets a written answer, so you always have a record.

Questions

Why no calls?

We work entirely by email so everything is in writing and you can reply when it suits you. Most small-business sites can be scoped from a short questionnaire.

Do I keep my domain and email?

Yes. Your domain stays registered in your name. When we launch, we change only the website records and leave your email (MX) settings alone.

Who owns the finished site?

Your content is yours. Once the refresh is paid in full, you can take the site files with you if you move to another provider.

Can you work with my WordPress, Wix or Squarespace site?

We rebuild sites as fast static sites hosted for you on Site Care. If you skip Site Care, you get the files and simple hosting steps. If you need to stay on your current platform, tell us and we'll say honestly whether we can help.

Do you build online stores or booking systems?

Not in the standard refresh. We can link to the store or booking tool you already use.

How are sites built?

We use templates and automated tools. Every page is checked with automated tests and against your written scope before you see it. We'll always tell you how something was made if you ask.

How do I pay?

By card through a secure payment link we email you after you approve the written scope. We never ask for card details by email.
