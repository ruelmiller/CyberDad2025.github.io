A monthly client newsletter on household and scam safety, ready to send under your agency's name

Each month you get one finished email issue and 8 social posts about the risks your clients' households actually run into: fake delivery texts, gift card scams, account takeovers, home inventory and more. You review it, edit anything you want, and send it from your own email tool.

What's included every month

  • One email issue as ready-to-paste HTML and plain text, about 600 words, with every statistic sourced to agencies like the FTC, FBI IC3, CISA and NAIC.
  • 8 social posts: captions plus 1080x1080 image cards with space for your logo, for Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram or Nextdoor.
  • Your branding: placeholders for your agency name, logo, agent name, phone and address. Founding members can send us their logo and details once, and we fill them in each month.
  • Delivered by email on the 1st of each month. You send it to your clients yourself. We never email your clients or touch your client list.
