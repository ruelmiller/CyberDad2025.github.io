Refund policy & FAQ

Our 30-day money-back guarantee

I want the CyberDad Shield Kit to genuinely help your family feel safer online. If it doesn't, for any reason, you can ask for a full refund of what you paid within 30 days of your purchase.

How to ask for a refund

Email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com within 30 days of your purchase date. Please include:

  • the email address you used at checkout, and
  • your name as it appears on the order.

A short note about what didn't work for you is welcome, but never required.

What happens next

  • You'll hear back within 1 business day to confirm we've received your request.
  • Your full refund of what you paid goes back to your original payment method.
  • We'll email you when your refund has been issued. Banks usually take about 5–10 business days to show it (this varies by bank).

The fine print (kept short)

  • The guarantee covers the CyberDad Shield Kit purchase price, one refund per customer per product.
  • Requests made more than 30 days after purchase are reviewed case by case.
  • Because the kit is a digital download, once you're refunded please delete your copy and don't share it.
  • File problems? If a file won't download or open, or seems incomplete, email us and we'll fix it or send a fresh copy. You don't need to ask for a refund to get help.

Frequently asked questions

Getting your kit
How do I get the CyberDad Shield Kit after I buy?

Right after checkout you'll get an email from ruel@getcyberdadkit.com with the subject "Your CyberDad Shield Kit is ready (download inside)". It has two download links: the full CyberDad Shield Kit and the 1-page 3 Fast Wins quick-start sheet.

I didn't get the email. What do I do?
  1. If your download email hasn't arrived, check your Spam/Junk folder and, in Gmail, the Promotions tab, or email us at ruel@getcyberdadkit.com.
  2. Search your inbox for "Shield Kit" or "getcyberdadkit".
  3. Still nothing? Email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com with the email address you used at checkout, and we'll send your downloads directly.

Tip: add ruel@getcyberdadkit.com to your contacts.

A download link doesn't work.

Try a different browser, or a computer instead of a phone. Still stuck? Reply to the delivery email or write to ruel@getcyberdadkit.com and I'll send a fresh link.

I typed the wrong email address at checkout.

Email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com from the address you want to use. Include the name on the order and the address you typed by mistake, and I'll get your kit to the right inbox.

Can I download it again later?

Yes. Keep the delivery email; its links are how you re-download, and we recommend saving the files to your phone or computer as soon as you get them. If you ever lose them, email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com and we'll send them again.

Do I need an account or a login?

No. The CyberDad Shield Kit is a digital download. There's no account to create and no password to remember.

Format and devices
What format is it in, and what do I need to open it?

Two PDFs (US Letter size): the CyberDad Shield Kit (51 pages) and the 3 Fast Wins quick-start sheet (1 page). Any phone, tablet or computer can open a PDF: iPhone/iPad (Files or Books), Android (Google Drive or the built-in viewer), Windows/Mac (your web browser or the free Adobe Acrobat Reader). You can print it at home, too.

What's inside?

The CyberDad Shield Kit (51 pages) opens with a short Start Here section, followed by 7 Quick-Action Modules:

  1. Password Manager Setup Guide
  2. Safe Browsing Rules
  3. WiFi Security Checklist
  4. Family Tech Agreement
  5. Social Media Safety Checklist
  6. Emergency Tech Plan
  7. Device Setup Guides

Each module starts with a 15-minute Quick Action, then walks you through the rest step by step, with checklists, templates and printables. It finishes with a bonus 30-Day Action Plan, a sources and verification appendix, and a plain-English glossary.

The separate 3 Fast Wins sheet (1 page) covers three simple protections you can switch on yourself: two-step verification for your main email, a family safe word, and phone updates plus theft protection.

Who it's for
Who is the CyberDad Shield Kit for?

Parents and caregivers who want to make their family's tech safer, with no technical background needed. It's written in plain English, one step at a time.

Is this a subscription?

No. The CyberDad Shield Kit is a one-time purchase. There are no recurring charges.

Can I share it?

Your purchase is for personal and family use, so use it with everyone in your household. Please don't resell, share or distribute it outside your family without written permission.
